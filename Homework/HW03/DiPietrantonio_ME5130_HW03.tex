\textbf{5--9, Solution:}

For a fixed box, we can write the mass balance as:

\begin{equation}
    \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int_{CV}\rho\,dV=-\oint_{CS}\rho\,(\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n)\,dS
\end{equation}

Since the probem is steady state, we can write:

\begin{equation}
    0=\oint_{CS}\rho\,(\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n)\,dS
\end{equation}

We should note that on verticle faces, $dA = dy$ and on horizontal faces, $dA = dx$. For face $A-B$, $u = U$ and $\mathbf{n} = -i$. Therefore:

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{V} \cdot \mathbf{n} &= -u 
                                &= -U
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

For face $B-C$, $\mathbf{n} = j$, therefore:

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{V} \cdot \mathbf{n} &= v
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

For face $C-D$, $u = u(y)$ and $\mathbf{n} = i$. Therefore:

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{V} \cdot \mathbf{n} &= u
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

and for face $D-A$, the plate is impermeable, so fluid cannot pass through it and thus $v = 0$. Nevertheless, we can now write our mass balance as:

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    0                           &= \int_0^y\rho\,(-U)\,dy+\int_{x_A}^{x_D}\rho\,v\,dx+\int_0^y\rho\,u\,dy+0 \\
    \int_{x_A}^{x_D}\rho\,v\,dx &= \int_0^y\rho U\,dy-\int_0^y\rho u\,dy \\ 
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

and further as:

\begin{equation}
\boxed{
    \int_{x_A}^{x_D}\rho\,v\,dx = \int_0^y(\rho U-\rho u)\,dy
}
\end{equation}

We can now write the momentum balance as:

\begin{equation}
    \sum F_x=\frac{1}{g_c}\oint_{CS}\rho\,u\,(\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n)\,dS
\end{equation}


For face $A-B$, the contribution is:

\begin{equation}
    \frac{1}{g_c}\int_0^y\rho\,U(-U)\,dy=-\int_0^y\frac{\rho UU}{g_c}\,dy
\end{equation}

For face $B-C$, the contribution is:

\begin{equation}
    \frac{1}{g_c}\int_{x_A}^{x_D}\rho\,U\,v\,dx=\int_{x_A}^{x_D}\frac{\rho\,v\,U}{g_c}\,dx
\end{equation}

For face $C-D$, the contribution is:

\begin{equation}
    \frac{1}{g_c}\int_0^y\rho\,u\,u\,dy=\int_0^y\frac{\rho uu}{g_c}\,dy
\end{equation}

and for face $D-A$, the contribution is $0$.
